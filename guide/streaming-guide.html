Stream Naija Draft Club on YouTube and TikTok

A free, step-by-step guide. Everything here is free: OBS Studio, YouTube Live and TikTok LIVE.

In short: install OBS on a laptop → show the game window + your camera → connect OBS to YouTube → press Start Streaming. For TikTok, first grow to 1,000 followers with short clips, then go LIVE.

What you need

  • A laptop or desktop computer (Windows or Mac) with a good internet connection.
  • Your webcam (most laptops have one) and a microphone or earphones with a mic.
  • A Google/YouTube account, and a TikTok account (you must be 18 or older to go LIVE on TikTok).

Step 1 — Install OBS Studio (free)

  1. Go to obsproject.com and download OBS Studio for your computer. Install it.
  2. When OBS opens for the first time, the Auto-Configuration Wizard appears: choose "Optimize for streaming" and click Next until it finishes.

Step 2 — Put the game and your face on screen

  1. Open naijadraftclub.com in Chrome, in its own window. Choose your level (Hard or Master looks great on stream).
  2. In OBS, at the bottom under Sources, click + → Window Capture (on Mac: macOS Screen Capture) → OK → choose the Chrome window with the game. The board appears in OBS.
  3. Click + again → Video Capture Device → choose your webcam. Drag the corners to make it small and put it in a corner.
  4. Optional: + → Text → type Play free at naijadraftclub.com and place it at the bottom.
  5. Speak and check that the green Mic/Aux bar moves in the Audio Mixer. If not, click the gear next to it and choose your microphone.

You play the game normally in Chrome. OBS only shows what is on that window, so viewers see every move live.

Step 3 — Go live on YouTube

  1. On youtube.com (on the computer), click the camera icon Create → Go live.
  2. The first time, YouTube asks you to verify your channel with your phone number. After that, live streaming can take up to 24 hours to be switched on. This is only the first time.
  3. In YouTube's live page (Live Control Room), choose Stream, give it a title (e.g. "Can anyone beat the Master? Naija Draft Club LIVE") and copy the Stream key.
  4. In OBS: Settings → Stream → Service: YouTube - RTMPS → paste the Stream key (or click Connect Account) → OK.
  5. In OBS click Start Streaming. After a few seconds YouTube shows your stream. You're live!

From a computer there is no minimum number of subscribers. From the YouTube phone app you need at least 50 subscribers to go live.

Step 4 — TikTok

  1. Grow your followers first: TikTok allows LIVE from 1,000 followers (in most countries) and age 18+.
  2. Record your best moments: in OBS click Start Recording while you play, then post short clips (15 to 60 seconds) of big multi-captures or beating the Master, with a caption like "Who fit beat this? Play free: naijadraftclub.com". Post often; that's how followers grow.
  3. For vertical videos (TikTok style): in OBS Settings → Video, set Base and Output resolution to 1080x1920 and rearrange the game and camera.
  4. When you reach 1,000 followers: go LIVE from the TikTok app, or use TikTok LIVE Studio (TikTok's free program for computers), which can show the game window just like OBS.

Streaming to both at the same time (optional)

When you're comfortable, you can stream to YouTube and TikTok at once with the free OBS plugin "Multiple RTMP outputs" or a service such as Restream (it has a free plan).

Tips for a great stream

  • Do a test first: on YouTube choose Unlisted so only you can see it.
  • Say the website often: naijadraftclub.com, and invite viewers to join the waiting list.
  • Explain your moves: why you capture backwards, when you're going for a king. Viewers love learning.
  • Challenge your viewers: "Beat the Master and send me your screenshot!"
  • Good light on your face, laptop on charge, and the game sound low so your voice is clear.
