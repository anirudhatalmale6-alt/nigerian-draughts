Rules (short)
  • Men move one square diagonally forward, but capture forward and backward.
  • Capturing is compulsory, and you must keep jumping while you can — but you may choose any capture, not necessarily the biggest.
  • Captured seeds stay on the board (faded) until your move is finished, and cannot be jumped twice.
  • A man reaching the far row (and stopping there) becomes a flying king: it moves and captures any distance along a diagonal.
  • You win when your opponent has no seeds or cannot move.
  • After each game the players switch colours, so the other player takes White and starts.
